I am a physics undergraduate student at Cambridge, currently working on astrophysics research.
In a previous life (before AI automated it) I was a programmer at numerous startups.
For a short time, I was even a Buddhist monk. Before then, I founded one of the first Bitcoin brokerages in the UK.
Outside of my professional life, I write poetry, perform spoken word, and relentlessly pursue self-development (with questionable success).
This is a place for me to organise my thoughts, and share things that have been valuable to me.
There's only so many times you can try to explain something, before it makes sense to write an article.
